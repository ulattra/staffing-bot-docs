# IMS Notifications - Terms of Service

IMS Notifications is an internal Google Chat app of Innowise, part of the Interview Management System (IMS). It is intended for Innowise employees only.

## Use of the app

- The app is meant for work tasks of Innowise employees related to recruiting and staffing.
- Use it in line with Innowise internal policies, including information security rules.
- Do not send the app passwords, payment details or any data unrelated to work.
- Copies of IMS notifications in direct messages are optional. You turn them on and off yourself in your IMS profile.

## Availability and changes

- The app is provided as is, without guarantees of availability or delivery time.
- Innowise may change, suspend or remove the app at any time.

## Contact

See the [Support page](support.md).
